Lichens is a platform that mining operators use to capture, collect and organize evidence (files, data) that are required for sustainability purposes, and Lichens then helps the user leverage their evidence to complete sustainability reporting requirents against standards such as Towards Sutainable Mining (TSM) and others.

What we at Northern Block have always struggled with is providing enough utility through a wallet interface for mining operators to convert their sustainability reporitng into digital credentials. They haven't seen the value proposition of using such a tool because there isn't any market demand for digital credential representations of their sustainability reports. Ensuring adherance to sustainability requirements is a cost to mining operators, and they are simply happy checking the box on these efforts. However, systems like Lichens make it easier for them to collaborate amongst representatives of their company to meet sustainability reporting compliance requirements. Theyt are already sold on these tools and Lichens already has 10 mining operators using their platform.

Meanwhile, we are seeing regulators in Canada and the EU pushing for more transparency in critical mineral supply chains. There are ongoing efforts amongst G7 countries right now to create traceability in dimaond supply chains through the Kimberly project to avoid diamonds from Russia that would be used to fueld conflicts. We're also seeing traceability requirements becoming a legal requirement in the EU in 2026, meaning that any Canadian exporter will need to demonstrate some level of traceability.

Digital credentials are a great solution for this. And through our involvement in the United Nations Transaprency Protocol, we're seeing how an architecture of various participants in a value chain issuing and publishing digital credentials to well known URLs can be accessed by third parties (regulators, buyers, investors) interested in traceability, and how these claims can also be linked together through the credential/data layer without any of them needing to be reported to a central platform. This architecture seems to be a scalabale one, prevents any forced use of single platforms (preventing vendor lock-in), and leverages cryptographic trust to sign verified data.

The pilot project Northern Block is now running with Lichens is to demonstrate how we can embed evidence-backed TSM+ credentialing directly in the Lichens user journey so mining operators can turn their completed TSM sustainability reports and supporting evidence into a digitally signed, verifiable credential—without adding UX friction. The result can be shared downstream (buyers, investors, partners) and published to the MAC Trust Registry to signal conformance and increase market confidence.
